Subject: Interested in contributing to The Junior’s Curve: How Coding Agents Change the Work of First-Year Developers?

Hi,

I’m starting a paper on this question: When first-year software developers maintain unfamiliar code, how does access to a coding agent affect what they spend time doing, the quality of the work, and what they can later do unaided? The draft lays out the study rather than reporting results.

I’d be interested in hearing from people who can help build comparable maintenance tasks, refine the work-phase rubric, or design a delayed exercise developers can do without an agent. If that sounds like something you’d like to work on, reply and tell me which part interests you.
