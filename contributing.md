# Contributing

Start with the [rough manuscript](paper/manuscript.md). Review the task design and phase rubric. Suggest synthetic maintenance tasks of comparable difficulty, acceptance-test criteria, or ways to make the unaided transfer exercise scoreable without seeing study condition.

If you have access to this repository, open an issue identifying the section, the problem or missing case, the evidence, and a proposed change. For edits, open a small pull request and link the issue. If you do not have access, reply to the invitation with what you would like to work on. An invitation to contribute is not an invitation to participate in a study.

Use synthetic examples or material you have permission to share. Do not upload private correspondence, raw job postings, identifying participant records, proprietary code, or credentials. Cite a primary source when one supports your point. Discuss roles and credit before taking on substantial work.
